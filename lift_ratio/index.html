Fractional habitat shares are interpolated. Actual visits require whole-habitat decisions. These scores are constructed for teaching, not held-out predictions.

The three datasets

  • Jigjiga-distribution synthetic example: 743 counts resampled from aggregate frequencies, with artificial scores. It shows a realistically skewed mix of counts and is not a model result.
  • Four-habitat arithmetic example: counts 40, 30, 20 and 10 with scores 4, 2, 3 and 1, small enough to check by hand.
  • Crossing-curves example: ten habitats with two different orders. A higher full-curve lift-ratio can still give worse coverage at a small budget.
